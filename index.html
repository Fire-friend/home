01 About Me

Ethos

I'm drawn to the quieter problems in vision — where data is imperfect, labels are few, and signal refuses to separate from noise. I believe elegance is a form of evidence: a method that can explain its own uncertainty is usually telling us something real.

My work treats restoration, matting, and semi-supervised learning as one question — how to recover a faithful world from partial measurement.
