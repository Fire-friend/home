Welcome to my homepage! 😄

I am a PhD candidate at Sun Yat-sen University (Shenzhen Campus), advised by Prof. Zhi Jin. Before that, I received my degree working on computer vision and pattern recognition.

My research interests center around low-level vision and learning with limited data, including image restoration, diffusion models, image matting, and semi-/self-supervised learning. Broadly, I am interested in how to recover faithful visual signals from partial and imperfect observations.

I am open to collaboration and always happy to discuss research via email.
